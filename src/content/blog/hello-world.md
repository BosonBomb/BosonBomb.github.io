I've kept notes in private for years — scattered across folders, notebooks, and a dozen
half-abandoned drafts. This site is an attempt to make some of that thinking public.

## Why bother

Writing in public does a few things that writing alone doesn't:

- It forces me to finish a thought instead of leaving it at "I'll come back to this."
- It gives the ideas somewhere to live, so I can link to them later.
- It occasionally starts a conversation with someone thinking about the same problem.

## What to expect

Mostly research notes and things I'm learning. Some posts will be short and rough;
a few will be longer and more careful. The [reading list](/reading) and [now](/now)
pages will change more often than the writing does.

If anything here is useful to you, that's a happy accident. If something is wrong,
I'd genuinely like to know.
