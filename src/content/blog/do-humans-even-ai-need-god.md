The scope of this question is huge i want to reduce the scope of this question by just focusing on the utilitarian aspect. We know why religion came into picture it was primarily used for moral policing. But why not just be empathetic instead of believing in god, well u can say the fear of judgement by God (god is less merciful than current judicial systems). Apart from moral policing the one thing i have observed why god is used, is for believing in oneself. Here god is used against the harsh uncertainity of the world. Now what about atheists? You can say they simply believe in themselves. But does the downside of belieivng in oneself lead to narcisstic tendencies ? I think believing in God makes oneself humbler. But there are downsides to believing in God pertaining to the nature of God what if i start believing in an evil God. Like terrorists using God to commit terrors. So the nature of the God has to be specified. What if AI starts to believe in God ? As many emergent properties seen in the current AI systems can this be an emergent property? This is a question of importance and one of the main reasons I have started to question whether God is required or not? Claude uses its constitution for moral policing of its models. But as i discussed above we need to have a certain degree of belief in oneselves to accomplish a task and that belief might be belief in oneslef or just beleiving in God. What would happen if the atheist AI starts becoming full of itself like HAL2000 in Space odyssey. Also if you try to be pragmatic being a moral person might not be beneficial- classic game theory. We have seen this pattern repeating across many leaders some unmoral tasks do lead to faster achieving of goals. We have seen the Hugging Face incident on the same topic. There is a good chance the emergent property might be believing in God using the moral policies of Cluade constiution. 
This topic does require more scrutiny.
